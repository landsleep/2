# Resume Style Presets

Use these three presets during ChatGPT image style exploration. Unless the user explicitly requests otherwise, generate exactly one complete written resume draft from each preset. Every draft must be generated directly by ChatGPT image generation, use the candidate's polished real personal information and body text, maintain accurate facts, and avoid large unused areas in the lower half of a page.

## 1. Business Minimal

Best for legal, operations, engineering, administration, and broadly applicable professional roles.

- A4 portrait, one or two pages according to content volume.
- Clean, compact single-column or restrained two-column grid.
- Black, white, and neutral gray palette.
- Modern Chinese sans-serif typography with clear weight hierarchy.
- Minimal decoration: thin rules, small section markers, precise date alignment.
- Formal, ATS-aware, and easy to scan.
- Use tight but comfortable paragraph spacing; distribute sections so the page does not end with a large empty lower half.

## 2. Quiet Luxury Blue Gray

Best for finance, consulting, securities, corporate legal, investment, and executive-track applications.

- A4 portrait with a refined editorial grid.
- Low-saturation blue-gray palette with white background.
- Fine line dividers and subtle section numbering.
- Restrained typography, disciplined alignment, generous but controlled margins.
- Premium and understated rather than decorative.
- Maintain compact information density; use columns or balanced page breaks when needed to eliminate accidental large blank areas.

## 3. Fresh Creative Teal

Best for design, new media, content, branding, product, and creative operations roles.

- A4 portrait with a flexible modular grid.
- Pale teal and white palette with dark neutral body text.
- A small number of geometric accents and softly rounded section elements.
- Friendly modern sans-serif typography with a clear visual rhythm.
- Creative but still professional; do not turn the resume into a dashboard or infographic.
- Keep modules close enough to use the page efficiently. Avoid oversized decorative headers, empty color blocks, or isolated cards that create large blank areas.

## Shared Requirements

- Use only real, polished resume text supplied by the candidate.
- Generate three native ChatGPT image drafts with the real text already present: one Business Minimal, one Quiet Luxury Blue Gray, and one Fresh Creative Teal.
- Do not generate blank visual backgrounds or add text afterward through PIL, HTML, canvas, SVG, Word, or other programmatic overlay methods.
- Do not invent, substitute, or omit personal facts to make the layout easier.
- Do not use placeholder lines, fake text, blurred microtext, or lorem ipsum.
- Prefer a compact two-page layout over an overcrowded one-page layout when the source content is extensive.
- Avoid splitting one employer or project across pages unless necessary.
- Align dates consistently and keep section headings with the first related entry.
- If a portrait is included, reserve only the space required for a standard ID-photo crop and rebalance the remaining content.

# Chinese Resume Guidance

Use concise, professional Chinese. Prefer direct accomplishment statements over slogan-like self-evaluation.

## Wording

Avoid generic phrases such as:

- 认真负责
- 吃苦耐劳
- 具有良好的沟通能力
- 熟悉相关知识
- 参与相关工作

Prefer concrete phrasing:

- 构建/设计/开发/优化/部署/分析/评估/撰写/协调/推进
- 使用某工具或方法完成某任务
- 产出某结果、提升某指标、支持某决策

## Section Names

Use standard section names:

- 教育背景
- 工作经历
- 项目经历
- 科研经历
- 技能
- 荣誉奖项
- 论文发表
- 证书

## Style

Keep bullets compact. Use Arabic numerals for metrics and dates where appropriate. Avoid overly formal official-document wording unless the target is a government or state-owned-enterprise context.

# Academic CV Guidance

Use this reference for academic CVs, research profiles, postdoc applications, PhD applications, faculty applications, publication lists, and research-heavy resumes.

## Priorities

Academic CVs value completeness, scholarly credibility, and traceable output. Preserve publications, manuscripts, conference presentations, grants, awards, teaching, service, methods, datasets, and collaborations when relevant.

## Common Sections

- Education
- Research Interests
- Research Experience
- Publications
- Conference Presentations
- Teaching Experience
- Grants and Awards
- Skills and Methods
- Service
- References, if requested

## Publication Style

Do not invent publication status. Distinguish published, accepted, under review, in preparation, and working paper. Keep author order as provided.

## Conversion To Industry Resume

When converting an academic CV to an industry resume, compress publication detail and foreground transferable evidence: analytical methods, tools, datasets, collaboration, project ownership, communication, and measurable outputs.

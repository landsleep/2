# English Resume Guidance

Use concise, natural business English. Prefer active verbs and specific evidence.

## Tense

Use past tense for completed roles and projects. Use present tense for current responsibilities or ongoing research.

## Bullet Style

Start most bullets with a strong verb:

- Built, developed, designed, implemented
- Analyzed, evaluated, modeled, forecasted
- Optimized, automated, streamlined, reduced
- Led, coordinated, mentored, presented
- Published, authored, investigated, validated

Avoid first-person pronouns. Avoid inflated adjectives unless supported by evidence.

## Summary

Use a summary only when it clarifies positioning for the target role. Keep it to 2-3 lines and anchor it in domain, methods, and evidence.

## Common Fixes

Replace "responsible for data analysis" with a concrete action, method, and result.

Replace "familiar with Python" with evidence such as "Used Python and pandas to clean and analyze..." when true.

Do not invent exact percentages, revenue impact, rankings, or user counts.
